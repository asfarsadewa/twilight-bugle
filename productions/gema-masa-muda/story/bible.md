# Story bible: Gema Masa Muda

A grown man remembers his school friends. He sings to the boy he was. The memory comes up around him on the stage, then goes down again. At the end he walks on.

The song came ready as an MP3. A male tenor sings it in Indonesian. It runs 5:25, with a key change into the final chorus and a slow fade.

## Cast

| Figure | Look and part |
|---|---|
| Dimas | The grown man, in his thirties. A white work shirt with the sleeves rolled up, a loose tie and a bag on his shoulder. He sings. |
| Young Dimas | Dimas at fourteen, in a junior high school uniform: a white shirt and navy shorts. |
| Sari | His friend. Two braids and a navy school skirt. He never finished the letter to her. |
| Ardi | His best friend. A cap turned backwards and a scraped knee. He went away. |
| The class | The classmates in one row, for the class photo |

## Motifs

- **The old desk.** Two names are scratched into it. It stands alone in the dark at the start and at the end.
- **The field.** It is gold with dust in the afternoon. At night it still shines in his heart.
- **The letter.** The envelope stays blank. Paper planes carry the letters to the future.
- **The flame tree.** A flamboyan stands at the edge of the field. Its red flowers fall in the final chorus.
