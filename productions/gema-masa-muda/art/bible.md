# Art bible: Gema Masa Muda

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it.

The style is the illustration of an old Indonesian school textbook of the 1980s and 1990s: gouache and ink on yellowed paper, printed with halftone dots and a little misregistration. It is the picture book the children had on their desks.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is printed like an illustration from an old Indonesian school textbook of the 1980s: warm gouache and ink drawn by hand, soft rounded shapes, gentle friendly faces with simple dot eyes, clean ink outlines, printed on slightly yellowed paper with visible halftone dots and a little colour misregistration at the edges, like an old offset print. Flat colour with soft gouache shading, warm afternoon light. Bold, clear shapes that read from the back row. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: old paper #EFE4C8, school white #F6F1E4, navy #2D3E66, school red #C7413A, flame red #D9452B, field ochre #D8A651, grass green #7A9A4A, sky blue #8FB4CF, ink #2B2620, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is warm, nostalgic and tender.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no characters, no letters, no numbers, no writing on blackboards or books or signs, no logos, no flags, no signature, no watermark. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish, no anime style.
