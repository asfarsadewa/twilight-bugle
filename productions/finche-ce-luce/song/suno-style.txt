Grand cinematic Italian operatic pop ballad, duet for a warm dark baritone (Matteo) and a clear aching mezzo-soprano (Lucia), with a large mixed choir. Minor key throughout, 66 BPM. The chorus is a simple, instantly memorable hook: the title "Finché c'è luce" sung on a soaring, long-held note, repeated, with the choir shouting back "torna a casa!" like a call across the sea. Short lines, strong repetition, one unforgettable melody that returns three times and grows each time. Full orchestra: low strings, solo cello, French horns, harp, timpani, tolling bell, with accordion and mandolin for the fishing village. The chorus is huge but grieving, never triumphant. Bridge: a driving 6/8 storm with timpani, brass and chanting choir. Then piano and a near-whisper. The final chorus is the biggest moment, sung through tears, ending on a minor chord, then solo cello and the sea. Sung in Italian.
