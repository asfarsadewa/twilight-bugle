# Art bible: Finché c'è luce

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. The beam of the lighthouse is the theatre's real spotlight in the haze.

The style is the Italian maritime ex-voto of the nineteenth century: the small painted panels that fishing families hung in harbour chapels after a storm. Naive, earnest tempera on wood, with flat seas of curling waves, small exact boats and people drawn with care. The panels usually show a holy figure in a corner and the letters "PGR". This production leaves out both: no religious figures and no writing.

STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is painted in the manner of an Italian maritime ex-voto of the 1800s: naive, earnest tempera on a small wooden panel, flat clear colour with simple modelling, precise little boats and rigging, curling stylised waves with white crests, sturdy fisherfolk with simple dignified faces, varnish cracks and a soft warm patina of age. Bold, clear shapes that read from the back row. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: limewash white #EFE7D6, sea green #2F5E5A, deep sea #1C3442, storm slate #4C5662, terracotta #B4593A, ochre #C8963E, faded red #9C3B2E, sail cream #E8DCC0, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is grief, endurance and work by the sea.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no letters, no numbers, no "PGR", no signature, no watermark. No religious figures, no saints, no Madonna, no crosses, no halos, no angels. No flags. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish, no anime style.
