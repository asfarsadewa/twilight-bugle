# Story bible: 少年回声

A grown man comes back to the basketball court of his old middle school at night. He finds the tin box that he and his friends buried under the plane tree when they were fourteen. Inside are the letters they wrote to their future selves. The memory comes up around him: the court in the gold dust, the old corridor, the bicycles at dusk, the green train that took a friend away. At the end, his old friends come back to the court, grown up, and they play one more game under the lights.

The song came ready as an MP3. A male voice sings it in Mandarin. It runs 3:40.

## Motifs

- **The tin box.** A biscuit tin with three letters to the future. They bury it in verse 1. He digs it up in the final chorus.
- **The basketball.** It passes from hand to hand. In the outro, the grown friends play with it again.
- **The court lights.** The court is gold with dust in the afternoon. At night it is the field of light in his heart.
- **The green train.** It takes Ah Hao away in the bridge.
