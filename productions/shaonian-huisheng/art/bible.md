# Art bible: 少年回声

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it.

The style is a gifted schoolboy's drawing in coloured pencil and blue ballpoint pen on a page of a Chinese exercise book of the 1990s, with the faint ruled lines of the paper. It is the drawing he made in the back of his homework book.

The school uniform is the plain blue-and-white tracksuit. No piece shows a red scarf, a flag, a badge or any slogan.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece looks like a gifted schoolboy's drawing on a page of a Chinese school exercise book of the 1990s: confident blue ballpoint pen outlines, soft coloured pencil hatching and shading, a little graphite, on off-white exercise paper with faint pale blue ruled lines across it. Warm and lively, with friendly faces drawn with care. Bold, clear shapes that read from the back row. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: exercise paper #F3F0E6, ballpoint blue #2F3F8F, pencil grey #6A6A6A, tracksuit blue #3C6DB4, tracksuit white #F5F5F0, court orange #D9874A, plane-tree green #6E9A4C, dusk gold #E8B25A, brick red #B5523E, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is warm, nostalgic and bittersweet.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no characters, no letters, no numbers, no Chinese characters, no writing on boards, books, envelopes or signs, no logos, no slogans, no flags, no red scarves, no badges, no signature, no watermark. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish, no anime style.
