# 山河为证

Biar Tanah Air Menjadi Saksi · Let the Land Bear Witness

A boy of sixteen joins the Long March of 1934 to 1935. He walks from the rivers of Jiangxi to the loess hills of the north. He loses his comrades on the way. He keeps walking for his country. The song is in Mandarin. Shitou sings it.

## Premise

Shitou ("Stone") is sixteen. He grows up in a village of red earth and rice fields in Jiangxi.

In October 1934 the army leaves Jiangxi. His mother weaves him a pair of straw sandals through the night. He sews a red star onto his cap. At the Yudu River no lamp burns. The villagers take down the doors of their houses to build the floating bridges. The army crosses in the dark.

The march is long. At the Xiang River the water turns red. At the Dadu River the planks of the Luding chain bridge are gone. The soldiers cross on the iron chains under fire. On the snow mountain the wind cuts like a knife. In the grassland the marsh swallows men. There is no food.

Old Zhao, the squad leader, gives Shitou his last ration. In the morning Zhao does not wake.

In the grassland at night, Shitou speaks to the comrades he has lost. He promises to see the Yellow River for them and to sing their names when spring comes.

In October 1935 the march reaches the north. Shitou has walked twenty-five thousand li. The mountains and rivers saw every step.

## The world

China in 1934 and 1935, along the route of the Long March. All places and dates are real.

| Stage | Place | Time |
|---|---|---|
| The start | The Yudu River, Jiangxi. The army crosses at night on bridges of door planks and boats. | October 1934 |
| The river of blood | The Xiang River, Guangxi. The heaviest losses of the march. | November and December 1934 |
| The chain bridge | The Luding Bridge on the Dadu River, Sichuan. Thirteen iron chains, with the planks removed. | May 1935 |
| The snow mountain | Jiajin Mountain, Sichuan. Thin air, summer clothes, deep snow. | June 1935 |
| The grassland | The marshes of the Songpan grassland. Hunger, cold and bog. | August 1935 |
| The arrival | The loess hills of northern Shaanxi | October 1935 |

The song stays with one soldier and his comrades. It names no leaders.

## Motifs

**The straw sandals.** His mother's gift. They wear through on the road, and he weaves new ones.

**The red star.** He sews it on at the start. It stays on his cap to the end.

**The door planks.** The village gives its doors to make the bridge. The people carry the march.

**The north.** The direction of the march, and of hope.

## Art direction

Proposed for the visual build, after the song: Chinese woodcut prints of the 1930s. Bold black carved lines, rough paper, and red as the only strong colour. The cyclorama gives the skies: dawn on the river, white snow, grey marsh, gold loess.
