# Art bible: 山河为证

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. Printed pieces keep bold, simple shapes, so no piece looks busy.

The style is a Chinese woodcut print of the 1930s, the art of the period itself. Black carved ink and warm paper carry it. One red is the only strong colour.

The imagery stays with the soldiers and the people of the march. It shows no political emblem except the plain red star on a cap. Flags are plain red. No piece shows a real person.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is printed as a Chinese woodcut print of the 1930s: bold black ink carved with knife and gouge, strong carved lines and hatching, chunky expressive shapes, the grain of the wood block showing in the solid blacks, printed on warm unbleached paper. Colour is rare: black ink and warm paper carry everything, with one strong red for caps, flags and a few accents, and a few muted touches of indigo grey and ochre. Bold, simple, monumental shapes with few small details: the piece must read from the back row and never look busy. Figures are sturdy and earnest, with simple carved faces, strong hands and steady eyes. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: ink black #171512, warm paper #E9DDC4, red #B3261E, indigo grey #3E4A5C, ochre #B98A3E, snow white #F2EEE4, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is solemn, firm and heroic, with grief and tenderness. Historical China, 1934 and 1935.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no characters, no letters, no numbers, no slogans, no logos, no signature, no watermark. No political emblems: no hammer and sickle, no portraits; a plain five-pointed red star on a cap is the only symbol. Flags are plain red cloth with no symbols. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish.
<!-- locked:end -->

## Cast

| Figure | Look |
|---|---|
| Shitou | A boy of sixteen. A grey-blue uniform too large for him, a soft cap with a red star, straw sandals, a cloth pack and a rifle taller than he is. |
| His mother | A village woman in a dark indigo jacket and a head cloth. She holds out a pair of straw sandals. |
| Old Zhao | The squad leader. A worn uniform, a grey stubble beard, a tin cup on his belt. He holds out a small cloth bundle of food. |
| The column | Soldiers in grey-blue with packs and rifles, and plain red flags |
| The villagers | Men and women carrying wooden house doors to the river |
| The fallen | Soldiers who stand in the mist, printed in grey |
