# Story bible: 형 먼저 가

June 1950, the first days of the Korean War. The monsoon rain falls. Two brothers from one village walk south with the refugees. Min is the younger, and Cheol is his hyung. At a wooden bridge over a swollen river, someone must stay and hold it while the others cross. Min stays. He tells his hyung to go first and to live.

Continuity: the user established them as blood brothers while developing the companion *형은 여기 있다 · Your Brother Is Here*.

The war takes them to different sides. In the last chorus they stand on the two banks of the river, and the bridge between them is gone. They promise to meet again at home.

The song is the theme of the film Hyeong in the sibling repository mini-mv-studio. The user extended it with Suno into this 3:47 cut. A young tenor sings Min. A deeper voice answers as Cheol in the final chorus, and both sing the last line.

The show depicts no violence. As in the film, the two sides are told apart only by their clothes: olive drab with steel helmets, or quilted mustard khaki with ear-flap caps. Each line carries one plain banner of unmarked cloth, blue or red. No insignia and no emblems.

## Cast

| Figure | Part |
|---|---|
| Min | Nineteen. Soaked white cotton work clothes and straw sandals. He stays at the bridge. He sings. |
| Cheol | Twenty-three, his hyung. A worn dark jacket and an A-frame carrier. He crosses. In the last chorus he stands across the river in olive drab, his helmet in his hand. |
| Their mother | Min's mother. She packs the rice balls. She waits at the open gate. |
| The road | Refugees with bundles and A-frame carriers. A column in olive drab marches north against them. |
| The two lines | A line in khaki with a red banner rises behind Min. A line in olive drab with a blue banner stands round Cheol. |

## Motifs

- **The rain.** It falls through the whole show. It stops on the last line.
- **The bridge.** Min holds it. Then he cuts it down so nobody can follow his hyung.
- **The rice ball.** Mother packed one. Min gave his half to Cheol.
- **The open gate.** The door of home stays open. Two boys walk in hand in hand.
