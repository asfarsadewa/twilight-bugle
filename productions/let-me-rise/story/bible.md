# Let Me Rise

Biar Aku Terbit · 如日初升

A young scribe paints the heroes of the temple wall. She wants a story of her own. The song is in English. Meret sings it.

## Premise

Meret is seventeen. She is the apprentice of the temple scribes in a small town on the great river.

Every day is the same. At dawn she fills the water jars at the river. At noon she copies hymns. At dusk she lights the lamps on the temple wall.

Meret paints the heroes of the old stories on the temple walls. She writes their names in gold leaf. Her own name is on no wall.

From the temple roof she watches the boats sail north to the sea. Her mother tells her to stay. The walls keep her safe.

On the night of the flood, the river rises. The festival barge breaks loose with the temple lamp on board. Meret takes the steering oar. She steers the barge through the dark flood. At dawn she brings the lamp home.

The town sees her as a hero. Meret does not stay. She takes a boat north at sunrise. The world is her stage now.

## The world

The town stands on the bank of a wide river, between green fields and the desert. Papyrus reeds grow thick along the water.

The temple has painted walls, lotus columns and a roof that looks over the river.

Every year the river floods. The town holds a festival on the night the water rises. A barge carries the temple lamp along the bank.

The Sky Mother is the night sky. She arches over the world in the shape of a woman made of stars.

The sun crosses the sky every day in a golden boat. It walks the same road every day. The whole world still waits for it to rise.

## Motifs

**Gold leaf.** Meret writes the names of heroes in gold. At the end the sunrise paints her in gold.

**The lamp.** The lamp is her routine. It is also the light she saves from the flood.

**The sun boat.** The sun keeps the same road every day and still rises like a hero. Meret wants to rise like the sun.

**The stage.** Meret wants to be the actor in the story. She does not want to paint it. The puppet theatre makes the wish literal.

## Art direction

Art Nouveau with Egyptian Revival motifs. The style uses flowing lines, halo arches behind figures, and ornamental frames of lotus and papyrus. The palette is lapis blue, turquoise, terracotta, papyrus cream and real gold leaf. Shapes are bold and calm, so every piece reads from the back row.

The look comes first. Every scene must be beautiful as a still.
