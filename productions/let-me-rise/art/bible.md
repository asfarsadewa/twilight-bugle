# Art bible: Let Me Rise

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. Far scenery is cut as flat-tone silhouettes. Painted pieces keep bold, simple shapes, so no piece looks busy.

The style is Art Nouveau with Egyptian Revival motifs. Flowing outlines, calm flat colour, lotus and papyrus ornament and gold leaf carry it.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from painted corrugated cardboard. Every piece is a flat cutout of cardboard about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is painted in opaque matte gouache in the style of an Art Nouveau poster with Egyptian Revival motifs: flowing outlines in a fine dark umber line, calm flat areas of colour with gentle gradients, lotus and papyrus ornaments, and a few touches of real gold leaf. Bold, simple shapes with few small details: the piece must read from the back row and never look busy. Figures have elegant, slightly long storybook proportions, simple painted faces with dark almond eyes lined in kohl, soft cheeks and a few confident brush lines. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: lapis blue #26457A, turquoise #3FA7A0, terracotta #C0643A, papyrus cream #EFE2C2, desert sand #D9B47A, lotus pink #E7A3A8, Nile green #5E7D4A, night indigo #1A2140, gold leaf #D9A441, with raw cardboard brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is tender, sentimental and heroic, like a beloved storybook staged with love. Ancient Egypt seen as a fairy tale.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no hieroglyphs, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no signature, no watermark. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish.
