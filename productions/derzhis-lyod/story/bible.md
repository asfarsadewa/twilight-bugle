# Story bible: Держись, лёд

Hold, Ice · Bertahanlah, Es · 冰啊，撑住

Show XVI of the Twilight Bugle. The story takes place in the siege of Leningrad, winter 1941 to spring 1942. A road of lanterns crosses the frozen lake. Trucks carry flour into the city on it. They carry children out. No real persons appear. The art shows no stars, flags or emblems. The trucks are unmarked.

Alyosha is nineteen. He drives a truck on the ice road. His mother cuts her bread ration into the share of his little sister, Masha. He comes home and finds his mother dead under her shawl. Her bread lies untouched. He takes Masha across the ice to the train on the far shore. Then he turns the truck back onto the thawing ice with flour, because the city still waits. In April the ice gives way under his truck. Years later, Masha stands on the shore of open water.

## Check against the brief

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is lost | The mother, Alyosha and the city's hungry winter. None of them comes back. |
| What they must still do | Drive one more run. Live on the far shore. |
| Where the light comes from | The mother gives her bread. Alyosha turns the truck back onto the ice. Masha waits all her life. |

## Cast

| Figure | Part |
|---|---|
| Alyosha | Nineteen. A quilted jacket, a fur cap with ear flaps and felt boots. He drives. He sings. |
| Masha | Nine, in a big shawl. In the last verse she is a grown woman in a grey coat. She sings the last verse. |
| Mother | A thin woman in an old shawl. She cuts the bread. |
| The lantern girl | A traffic guard on the ice. She swings a lantern on the beat. |
| The convoy | Unmarked trucks with open doors. Sacks of flour. Children wrapped in blankets. |

## Art

Russian lacquer miniature in the Palekh manner: a black lacquer ground, fine gold hatching and jewel-bright tempera. The ice is white and silver on the black. The lanterns and the cab light are the only gold flames.

## Motifs

- **The bread.** 125 grams on the scale. The mother's share goes to Masha.
- **The open door.** The drivers keep the cab door open on the ice.
- **The lanterns.** They mark the road. They go out one by one in the thaw.
- **"One more run."** The hook. Alyosha says it each time he turns back.
