Grand cinematic Russian orchestral ballad, sung in Russian by a warm young male baritone (Alyosha) and, in the last verse, a clear grown female soprano (Masha), with a deep Russian men's choir and a women's choir. Minor key throughout, 70 BPM. The chorus is a simple, unforgettable hook, "Держись, лёд, держись", on a soaring long-held note, the choir answering "Держись, держись". Short lines, strong repetition, one melody that returns and grows. Orchestra with bayan accordion, balalaika tremolo, domra, low strings, solo cello, French horn, timpani and a deep bell. The pre-chorus is a driving engine pulse: a low string ostinato under a men's choir chant. The bridge is the spring thaw: pounding timpani, full strings, a brass swell and a chanting choir. Then everything drops to wind and one bayan chord. The final chorus is the biggest moment, sung through tears, and ends on a minor chord, then a lone bayan.
