# Art bible: Держись, лёд

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. The snow is the theatre's own snow effect. The lantern flames, the cab light and the lamp are the theatre's warm practical light.

The style is the Russian lacquer miniature in the Palekh manner. Figures are painted in egg tempera on a black lacquer ground. They are slender and graceful. Fine gold hatching lights the folds, the snow and the ice. The colours are deep and bright against the black.

The figures and the trucks are invented. No piece shows a real person. No piece shows a star, a flag, a banner, an insignia, a portrait, a church dome, a cross or a weapon. The trucks are unmarked.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from painted card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is painted in the manner of a Russian Palekh lacquer miniature: slender, graceful figures in fine egg tempera, deep glossy black lacquer showing between and around the shapes, fine gold-leaf hatching on the folds of the clothes, on the snow and on the ice, small precise brushwork, jewel-bright colour against the black. Bold, clear silhouettes that read from the back row. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: lacquer black #0E0C0B, gold leaf #C9A04A, ice white #E9ECEE, frost blue #7FA3BF, deep night blue #1F3550, birch white #EDE6D6, ochre #B8862E, faded crimson #9A3A34 used only in small touches of clothing, warm lamp gold #F2B85A used only for flames and lit windows, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is hunger, deep cold, endurance and a brother's love for his little sister.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no letters, no Cyrillic, no numbers, no signature, no watermark. No five-pointed stars, no red stars, no hammer and sickle, no flags, no banners, no insignia, no badges, no medals, no portraits of leaders, no church domes, no crosses, no icons, no religious figures, no weapons, no tanks, no aircraft. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no anime style, no cartoon style.
<!-- locked:end -->
