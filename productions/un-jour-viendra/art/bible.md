# Art bible: Un jour viendra

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. Painted pieces keep bold, simple shapes, so no piece looks busy.

The style is a solemn Chinese ink painting. Ink, mist and empty paper carry it. Vermilion, plum pink and gold leaf are the only strong colours.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from painted card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick rice-paper card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is painted as a classical Chinese ink-wash painting: wet and dry black ink on warm rice paper, soft graded washes, confident calligraphic brush strokes, mist and empty space. Colour is rare and quiet: pale ink grey and misty blue-grey, with only a few accents of vermilion red, plum blossom pink and real gold leaf. Bold, simple shapes with few small details: the piece must read from the back row and never look busy. Figures have elegant, slightly long storybook proportions, simple brush-drawn faces with dark almond eyes, and a few confident lines. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: ink black #1B1A1A, ink grey #6E6A66, mist blue-grey #9AA6AD, rice paper cream #EFE6D2, vermilion #C23B22, plum pink #E3A0A8, pine grey-green #5F6E5E, gold leaf #D4A84A, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is solemn, tender and heroic, like a beloved legend painted on a scroll. Mythical ancient China.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no calligraphy characters, no letters, no numbers, no seals with characters, no logos, no signature, no watermark. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish.

## Cast

|---|---|

## Composition rules

| Asset | Composition |
|---|---|
| Key art | The whole stage inside a toy theatre, seen from the audience |
| Flat, prop, puppet part | One single cutout seen straight from the front, on a transparent background. No shadow, no stand, no rod, no other piece. |
