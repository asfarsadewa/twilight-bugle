# Art bible: Bintang Senja

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. Far scenery is cut as flat-tone silhouettes. Painted pieces keep bold, simple shapes, so no piece looks busy.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from painted corrugated cardboard. Every piece is a flat cutout of cardboard about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is painted in opaque matte gouache with bold, simple shapes, large calm areas of colour, visible brush strokes and a few touches of real gold leaf. Few small details: the piece must read from the back row and never look busy. Figures have elegant, slightly long storybook proportions, simple painted faces with dark almond eyes, soft rosy cheeks and a few confident brush lines. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: dusk rose #E9A7A0, amber #E89B4A, dusk blue #2E3A66, pale gold #F2D27A, star white #FFF6DA, rust red #A34A2A, brass #C8963E, reed olive #6E7A4A, stone grey #8C8F99, with raw cardboard brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is tender, sentimental and heroic, like a beloved fairy tale staged with love. A frontier fantasy that belongs to no real culture.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no signature, no watermark. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish.
<!-- locked:end -->

## Cast

| Figure | Look |
|---|---|
| Bayu | Young man, the bugler of a frontier fort. Short dark hair, a kind, determined face. A rust red cloak, plain brown leather armour, a short sword at his hip. A brass bugle on a cord. |
| Kirana | A fallen star in the shape of a young woman. Skin of pale warm gold. Long hair of pale gold light that streams behind her like the tail of a comet. A simple gown of dusk blue that fades to gold at the hem. |
| The Hollow Hounds | Wolves of shadow: black silhouettes with ember eyes. |

## Places

| Place | Look |
|---|---|

| The valley | Tall reeds and a slow river below the fort. |
| The Stair of Winds | One tall, steep mountain peak with a zigzag path. Snow near the top. |
| The summit | A rough rock outcrop at the top of the mountain. |

## Composition rules

| Asset | Composition |
|---|---|
| Key art | The whole stage inside a toy theatre, seen from the audience |
| Flat, prop, puppet part | One single cutout seen straight from the front, on a transparent background. No shadow, no stand, no rod, no other piece. |
