# Bintang Senja

暮星 · The Evening Star

A star falls at twilight. A young bugler carries her back to the sky. He loses her to save her. The song is in Indonesian. The star sings it.

## Premise

Bayu is the bugler of Emberhill, a fort on the frontier. He sounds the call at dawn and at dusk.

One dusk, a star falls into the valley below the fort. Bayu finds her in the reeds. She is a girl of pale gold light. Her name is Kirana.

On earth, a star fades a little each day. Kirana can live only in the sky.

The sky opens once a year, on the last twilight, at the summit of the Stair of Winds. It stays open for one call of a bugle.

Bayu carries Kirana up the mountain. The Hollow Hounds hunt them. The hounds eat starlight.

At the summit, Bayu lets her go. Kirana rises and becomes the evening star.

Every dusk after, Bayu plays on the wall of the fort. The evening star shines brightest while he plays.

## The world

Emberhill is a stone fort on a hill above a valley of reeds and a slow river.

The Stair of Winds is a mountain north of the fort. Its path climbs through pines into snow and bare rock.

The Hollow Hounds are wolves of shadow with ember eyes. Light from a star draws them. The call of a bugle scatters them.

## Motifs

**The bugle.** The bugle is Bayu's voice. In the song, the trumpet answers Kirana's lines and plays the solo.

**Dusk.** The story begins and ends at twilight. The sky on the cyclorama moves from day to dusk to night and back to dusk.

**Fading light.** Kirana's light dims a little in each scene on earth. It returns in full when she rises.
