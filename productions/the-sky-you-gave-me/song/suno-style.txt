Majestic Mandarin symphonic ballad, slow swaying 6/8. One adult female lead: warm intimate verses, clear natural Mandarin diction, supported soaring choruses and stable sustained notes. Compose the chorus first: an unforgettable four-bar title hook on “你给我的星空”, a yearning upward leap answered by a falling phrase. Introduce that melody on solo cello and repeat it recognizably in every chorus. G-minor warmth, descending lament bass, rich suspended chords, minor-subdominant colour and late dominant resolutions. Grand piano, solo cello, oboe, lush divided strings, French horns and harp; restrained timpani and cymbal swells at the orchestral peak. A coal miner's daughter remembers his blackened hands, his loss of sight and the life he made possible. The final chorus expands in the same key, then falls back to piano and cello as she traces stars into his palm. Tenderness, grief, gratitude and quiet dignity. Fully sung, one voice, orchestral accompaniment.
