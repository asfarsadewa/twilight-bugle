# The Daybreak Vow

天明之约 · Janji Fajar

A prince and a princess sing a Mandarin duet. A war keeps them apart. The video is a cardboard puppet theatre.

## Premise

The Hollow Crown brings the Long Night to the kingdom of Ilvara. Dawn has not come for a hundred nights.

Prince Aurel leads the army to the northern front. Princess Ilune holds the walled city behind him.

Each night, Ilune lights the Moonlamp on the Lantern Spire. The lamp tells Aurel that the city still stands.

They promise to meet again at daybreak.

## The world

Ilvara is a walled city with nine gates. The Lantern Spire stands at the center of the city.

The Moonlamp burns at the top of the Spire. It draws its light from the moon.

The Sunward Marches lie north of the city. Snow covers the passes for most of the year.

The Hollow Crown lives beyond the northern ice. It is a crown with no king.

Black strings hang from the Hollow Crown. The strings move its soldiers, the Umbral Host.

The Hollow Crown swallowed the sun. The Long Night started on that day.

## Motifs

**Sun and moon.** The sun and the moon share the sky at dawn. Aurel and Ilune meet again at dawn.

**Sword and lamp.** The chorus pairs them. Aurel sings "My sword rings out for you." Ilune sings "My lamp burns for you."

**Do not look back.** Ilune says this line at the gate in verse 1. In verse 2, the far lamp says it to Aurel.

**Strings.** The Umbral Host hangs on strings. Aurel and Ilune are rod puppets with no strings. In the bridge, the strings of the Hollow Crown seize Ilune. In the final chorus, Daybrand cuts the strings. Ilune falls free.

## Theatre

This section is a first draft. The visual build starts after the song review.

- The stage is a proscenium theatre in 3D. The camera can move inside it.
- Each figure is a flat cardboard cutout. Its corrugated edge shows when it turns.
- Figures move on rods from below. Some figures slide in slots in the stage floor.
- Painted flats make the backdrops. Stagehands change them on rails.
- Footlights, a follow spot and lamps on the set light the stage.
- The Hollow Crown is the only figure on strings.
