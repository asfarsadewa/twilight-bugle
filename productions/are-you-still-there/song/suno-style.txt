Majestic, deeply melodic Korean orchestral ballad. One mature male baritone, warm low verses and a ringing, sustained upper register. A solitary older brother calls his missing younger brother across a river at dusk, decades after a war separated them. Grand piano, solo cello and oboe, divided symphonic strings, French horns, harp, restrained timpani and cymbal swells. An original recurring tragic theme, stated first on piano and cellos. A simple four-bar chorus hook, repeated recognizably. Minor-key harmony, descending lament bass, minor subdominant, half-diminished approaches and dominant flat-nine tension. Intimate melodic verses, late-resolving suspensions and a large, memorable refrain. Pull back for the memory of June rain, then build to an immense final chorus and return to a quiet, unanswered ending. Natural Korean diction, long legato lines, controlled vibrato, family love and regret. Same-key final chorus, a solitary ending. No second lead, spoken narration, marching snare or electronic beat.
