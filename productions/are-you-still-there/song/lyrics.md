# 형은 여기 있다 · Your Brother Is Here

Kakak Ada di Sini · 哥在这里

Cheol sings every line. Min does not answer. English and Indonesian translations follow each Korean line.

## Intro

*Instrumental.*

## Verse 1

**해 질 무렵 그 강가에 서면**  
When I stand by that river at dusk  
Saat senja, aku berdiri di tepi sungai itu

**저편 우리 마을에 불이 켜진다**  
Lights come on in our village across the water  
Lampu menyala di kampung kita di seberang

**내 머리는 어느새 하얘졌는데**  
My hair has turned white before I knew it  
Tanpa terasa rambutku telah memutih

**기억 속의 너는 아직 열아홉**  
In my memory, you are still nineteen  
Dalam ingatanku, kau masih sembilan belas

## Pre-chorus 1

**강바람에 갈대만 스쳐도**  
At the slightest river wind through the reeds  
Angin sungai baru menyentuh ilalang

**내 이름 부르던 네 목소리**  
I hear the voice that used to call my name  
Terdengar suaramu yang memanggil namaku

## Chorus 1

**민아, 아직 거기 있니**  
Min, are you still there?  
Min, masihkah kau di sana?

**저녁밥 짓는 연기 아래**  
Beneath the smoke of supper fires  
Di bawah asap tungku makan malam

**형 먼저 가라던 그 한마디**  
Those words of yours: Brother, go first  
Kata-katamu: Kak, pergilah dulu

**아직도 나를 붙잡는구나**  
Still hold me where I stood  
Hingga kini masih menahanku

**네가 없는 집은 집이 아니더라**  
Without you, home never felt like home  
Tanpamu, rumah tak lagi terasa rumah

**동생아, 내 목소리 들리니**  
Little brother, can you hear my voice?  
Adikku, terdengarkah suaraku?

## Verse 2

**장맛비 쏟아지던 그날 아침**  
That morning when the monsoon rain poured down  
Pagi itu, hujan musim panas tercurah

**네 몫의 밥을 내 손에 쥐여 주고**  
You pressed your own share of rice into my hand  
Kau letakkan jatah nasimu di tanganku

**내 손을 놓으며 웃던 그 얼굴**  
That face that smiled as you let go of my hand  
Wajahmu tersenyum saat melepas tanganku

**내가 돌아선 뒤 울진 않았을까**  
After I turned away, did you perhaps begin to cry?  
Setelah aku berpaling, mungkinkah kau menangis?

## Pre-chorus 2

**널 지키겠다던 형의 약속인데**  
It was your older brother's promise to protect you  
Kakaklah yang berjanji akan menjagamu

**왜 네가 날 지켜야만 했나**  
Why did you have to be the one to protect me?  
Mengapa justru kau yang harus menjagaku?

## Bridge

**혹시 너도 이 노을을 보니**  
Could you be watching this same sunset?  
Mungkinkah kau juga melihat lembayung ini?

**저녁이면 내 이름을 부르니**  
When evening comes, do you call my name?  
Saat senja, apakah kau memanggil namaku?

**살아서 집에 가라던 네 말에**  
Because you told me to stay alive and go home  
Karena katamu: Hiduplah, pulanglah

**난 오늘도 살아 여기 서 있다**  
I am alive and standing here again today  
Hari ini aku masih hidup, berdiri di sini

## Orchestral lift

*Instrumental.*

## Final chorus

**민아, 아직 거기 있니**  
Min, are you still there?  
Min, masihkah kau di sana?

**형은 여기서 널 기다린다**  
Your brother is waiting for you here  
Kakak menunggumu di sini

**늦어서 미안하다, 내 동생아**  
I am sorry I am so late, my little brother  
Maaf aku terlambat, adikku

**이번엔 형이 남아 있을게**  
This time your brother will stay behind  
Kali ini, biar kakak yang tinggal

**해가 지면 춥다, 내 동생아**  
It gets cold after sunset, my little brother  
Selepas matahari terbenam, dingin, adikku

**집에 가자, 우리 함께 가자**  
Let us go home; let us go together  
Mari pulang, kita pulang bersama

## Outro

**대답 없는 강 위로 해는 저물고**  
The sun goes down over the river that gives no answer  
Matahari tenggelam di atas sungai yang tak menjawab

**민아, 형은 여기 있다**  
Min, your brother is here  
Min, kakak ada di sini
