# Art bible: 형은 여기 있다 · Your Brother Is Here

Version 1. Every new image request starts with the locked text below. The companion carries Hyeong's charcoal and ink on hanji into a dusk washed with amber. The cold, rain-washed pieces from Hyeong return only as memories; their reuse is deliberate continuity.

The sky is always the theatre's lit cyclorama. Generated scenery has transparent sky above its cut edge. Light, rain, haze and smoke belong to the stage.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife, with raw brown card at the edges. The face is painted in soft charcoal line and grey ink wash with thin watercolour on warm Korean hanji mulberry paper, its long fibres visible. Loose expressive charcoal strokes and quiet, tender faces. Broad, calm shapes readable from the back row. Puppet joints are round brass split pins. The present day has the last amber and persimmon light of dusk over indigo-grey water; the memories are cool and rain-washed. Palette: hanji cream #EDE6D6, charcoal #2B2A28, slate #5E6670, mist #C9CDD0, earth #6E5A44, indigo #3B4A66, pine #4E5E48, persimmon #B4553A, dusk amber #D6A365, raw card #A57E52. The mood is family love, regret and unanswered longing in Korea, many years after a separation in June 1950.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, writing, lettering, numbers, logos, signature or watermark. No national flags, insignia, emblems, armbands or badges. No violence or weapons in use. No real persons. No photographic people, plastic, glossy finish, anime style or sculpted 3D characters. The illustration must remain a painted, flat card cutout. The rods, strings, light and sky will be added by the theatre.

## Cast and continuity

- **Older Cheol:** the same strong, kind face as the young Cheol in Hyeong, now lined, with short white hair. A worn grey-brown coat over a cream shirt, faded indigo scarf, dark trousers and worn shoes. He stands slightly stooped. One hand rests on a plain wooden cane; the other can reach across the river. No medals or uniform.
- **Young Cheol and Min:** Hyeong's original jointed card marionettes, with their existing rig calibration. They are memories, never an answering presence in the present-day village.
- **Mother and the two boys:** Hyeong's original slide cards, used in the memories of food, home and childhood.

## Set

The near riverbank is downstage, with reeds and a broken wooden bridgehead. Two river ground rows rock in opposite phase. Far upstage, the village has a few amber windows and a chimney. Old scenery flies or slides away for the June-rain memory. The same river remains through both times.

New art: the older Cheol puppet, the near bank, reeds, the present-day village, the empty bridgehead, a rice bundle, key art and a house-wall poster. Each new piece uses the key art as its style reference. Each is generated separately with the built-in image tool; prompts and receipts are retained.

The poster leaves plain bands above and below for the theatre's printed titles. No generated lettering.

## Physical rules

Puppets hang from head and hand strings. Flats fly, scenery slides on visible wires, props rise on rods. Transparent cutouts receive traced solid outlines and paint relief through `tools/cutout.py`. Puppet pin coordinates are calibrated against the actual generated parts, never guessed from a prompt. New set pieces are checked as rendered stills before the full video.
