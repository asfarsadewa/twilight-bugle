# Art bible: ふるさとが見える

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. The snow is the theatre's own snow effect. The farmhouse lamp and the dawn are the theatre's real lights.

The style is the Meiji colour woodblock print, nishiki-e, of the 1880s and 1890s. The new aniline red and Prussian blue of that time give hard, bright colour against black key lines. Colour areas are flat, with bokashi gradations in the sky and the snow. The wood grain and the paper show through. Prints of that time carry cartouches, seals and the names of the artist and the publisher. This production leaves out all writing and all seals.

The figures and the mill are invented. No piece shows a real person, a real company or its emblem. No piece shows a flag or a rising-sun disc with rays.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is printed in the manner of a Japanese Meiji-era colour woodblock print, nishiki-e, of the 1880s: strong black key-block outlines, flat areas of colour, bokashi gradations, visible wood grain and soft washi paper texture, the vivid new aniline red and deep Prussian blue of that era beside soft indigo and grey. Figures have the simple, graceful faces of Meiji prints. Bold, clear shapes that read from the back row. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: aniline red #C8283A, Prussian blue #1F3F78, indigo #2B3550, snow white #F2EFE6, washi cream #E9DFC8, pine green #3E5B3F, ink black #1C1A1A, ochre #C79A4A, with raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is grief, cold, endurance and love between a brother and a sister.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no letters, no numbers, no kanji, no kana, no cartouches, no title boxes, no seals, no signature, no watermark. No flags, no rising sun with rays, no military, no company emblems, no religious figures. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish, no anime style, no manga style.
