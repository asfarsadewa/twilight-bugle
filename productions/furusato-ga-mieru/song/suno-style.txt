Grand cinematic Japanese ballad, sung in Japanese by a clear, fragile young female lead (Hana) and a deep, warm male voice (Kichi), with a women's choir. Minor key and pentatonic throughout, 70 BPM. The chorus is a simple, unforgettable hook: "ああ ふるさとが見える" sung on a soaring, long-held note, with the choir answering softly "見える 見える". Short lines, strong repetition, one melody that returns and grows. Orchestra with shakuhachi, koto, shamisen, taiko, low strings, solo cello and wind. The mill song is a driving work chant on taiko and shamisen. The bridge is a blizzard climb: pounding taiko, full strings and a chanting choir. Then everything drops to piano, wind and a whisper. The final chorus is the biggest moment, sung through tears, and ends on a minor chord, then a lone shakuhachi.
