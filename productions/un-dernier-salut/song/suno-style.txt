French tragic orchestral duet, a farewell lament. Slow, grave 3/4 around 82 BPM, D minor throughout. Deep melancholy from first note to last. Two adult leads: expressive female mezzo-soprano and warm male baritone, both with restrained grief, longing and clear natural French diction. A haunting four-bar chorus on “Un pas pour toi, un pas pour moi”: a reaching phrase followed by a long aching descent. Keep its melody when “pour” becomes “vers”. Piano, solo cello, low strings, English horn and dark French horns; full strings and restrained timpani build tragic intensity. Descending minor bass, chromatic inner voices, suspensions and delayed resolutions. Maintain a measured triple pulse and instrumental passages for puppet movement. The finale grows in pain and resolve, stays minor, then ends on solitary cello and a quiet bare minor chord. No comic vocals, fairground bounce, accordion, pizzicato, bright major-key lift, choir or triumphant ending.
