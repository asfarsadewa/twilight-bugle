CHORUS 1 — rivalry

A step for you, a step for me.
Each in their place, each by their own rules.
Turn, turn, one more turn.
Move over, it is my turn!
As long as a little stage floor remains,
There is still a whole world to set dancing.
One last turn, one last bow,
For this theatre that will never open again.

CHORUS 2 — trust

The opening becomes "A step towards you, a step towards me."
They ask each other to keep their balance, listen, and lean together.
The last four lines stay the same, as does the recurring melody.

FINAL CHORUS — partnership

A step towards you, a step towards me.
Turn with me, stay with me.
Turn, turn, one more turn.
Daybreak can wait its turn.
The last four lines stay the same. Their theatre still closes.

THE LAST EXCHANGE

He prepares to return quietly backstage with his broom.
She asks him to put it down and stay: tonight the bow will be for him.
They finish with the title phrase and bow together.
