# Story bible: Cùm an Teine

Keep the Fire · Jagalah Api · 守住炉火

Show XV of the Twilight Bugle. The story takes place in the Scottish Highlands in 1845, in the Clearances. No real persons appear. No estate or glen is named. The art shows no clan tartans, crests or emblems.

A peat fire has burned in one family's hearth for two hundred years. The estate clears the glen for sheep. Seonag gives her son Ewan the last ember in an iron pot. She dies on the shore before the ship sails. In the storm at sea the ember goes out. On a cold new shore, Ewan and Morag light a new fire with flint and their own hands.

## Check against the brief

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is lost | Seonag, the glen and the two-hundred-year fire. None of them comes back. |
| What they must still do | Cross the sea. Build a house where there is nothing. |
| Where the light comes from | Ewan holds the pot above the waves. Two pairs of hands guard one spark. The new fire is not the old fire. |

The new fire is not a reward. The empty glen closes the show.

## Cast

| Figure | Part |
|---|---|
| Ewan | Twenty-six. A crofter in a grey plaid and a bonnet. He cuts peat. He carries the ember pot. He sings. |
| Morag | Twenty-four, his wife. A brown shawl. She beats cloth with the women. She holds the baby. She strikes the flint. She sings. |
| The baby | A small bundle in Morag's arms |
| Seonag | Ewan's mother. A white mutch cap and a black shawl. She banks the fire every night. She gives the ember. |
| The women | The waulking circle at the board |
| The factor's men | Plain dark coats and top hats. They nail the notice and pull the rooftree down. They have no faces in the art. |
| The glen folk | A line of families with bundles and creels on the shore road |

## Motifs

- **The fire.** It burns for two hundred years. It travels in an iron pot. The sea puts it out. A new one starts from a spark.
- **The hearthstone.** It comes out of the ruin. It goes on Seonag's grave.
- **The waulking song.** The work chant of the glen. It comes back as the storm chant.
- **The empty glen.** Roofless walls in the rain. The last image.

## Scene plan

| Section | Stage |
|---|---|
| Intro | Wind over the sea. Mist comes off the glen. One chimney smokes. |
| Verse 1 | The house. Seonag banks the fire. Ewan cuts peat on the hill. |
| Pre-chorus | The waulking board. The women beat the cloth on the beat. |
| Verse 2 | The notice on the door. The men pull the rooftree down. The thatch falls. Seonag puts the ember in the pot. |
| Chorus 1 | The shore road. The glen walks to the bay. The ship waits. |
| Verse 3 | Night on the shore. Seonag dies in her sleep. They lay the hearthstone on her cairn. |
| Chorus 2 | Dawn. The boats take them out to the ship. The cairn stays on the shore. |
| Bridge | The storm. The ship pitches on the cloth sea. Ewan holds the pot above the waves. A wave goes over. The ember goes out. |
| Break | Dark. Wind. One harp note. |
| Verse 4 | Snow on a new shore. No roof. Morag strikes the flint again and again. Ewan cups his hands. One spark catches. |
| Final chorus | The new fire. They kneel at it with the baby. Snow falls. |
| Outro | The glen across the sea. Roofless walls in the rain. No smoke. |
