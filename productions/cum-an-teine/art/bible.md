# Art bible: Cùm an Teine

Version 1. Every image request for this production starts with the locked text below. A change to the locked text makes version 2.

The sky never comes from a painting. The theatre's cyclorama lights it. The snow is the theatre's own snow effect. The ember, the hearth fire and the new fire are the theatre's warm practical light.

The style is the Victorian wood engraving of the 1840s illustrated newspapers, tinted by hand with thin watercolour. Dense black engraved line gives the form. Fine parallel hatching and cross-hatching give the shade. Thin washes of peat brown, sea grey, heather and moss lie over the line. The paper is soft and off-white. Only the fire is a strong warm orange.

The figures, the glen and the estate are invented. No piece shows a real person. No piece shows a clan tartan, a crest, a flag, a saltire or a religious figure. The plaids are plain homespun in grey and brown.

## Locked text

<!-- locked:start -->
STYLE: A grand hand-made toy theatre production built from printed card. Every piece is a flat cutout of thick card about 4 mm thick, cut by hand with a craft knife. The front of each piece is printed in the manner of a Victorian wood engraving from an 1840s illustrated newspaper, then tinted by hand with thin transparent watercolour: dense black engraved lines, fine parallel hatching and cross-hatching for shade, soft off-white rag paper, washes that sit loosely over the lines. Figures have the plain, dignified faces of 1840s engravings of Highland crofters. Bold, clear shapes that read from the back row. Puppet joints are brass split pins. Palette: peat brown #5A3E2B, sea grey #6E7C80, slate blue #3B4A5C, heather #7D5A7A, moss green #5E6B3A, bone white #EEE8DA, ink black #1A1817, with ember orange #D9772B used only for fire and glowing peat, and raw card brown #A57E52 at cut edges. The mood is grief, cold, wind, endurance and a family's love.
CONSTRAINTS: No text, no letters, no numbers, no captions, no signature, no watermark, no engraver's initials. No tartan patterns, no clan crests, no flags, no saltire, no crosses, no religious figures, no military. No photographic people, no 3D render look, no plastic, no glossy finish, no anime style, no cartoon style.
