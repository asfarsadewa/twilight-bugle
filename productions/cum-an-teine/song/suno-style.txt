Grand cinematic Scottish Celtic ballad in English with a Gaelic refrain, sung by a warm young male tenor (Ewan) and a clear female alto (Morag), with a Gaelic women's choir. Dorian and Aeolian minor throughout, 68 BPM. The chorus is a simple, unforgettable hook, "Keep the fire, keep the fire burning", on a long rising note, the choir answering softly "Cùm an teine". Short lines, strong repetition, one melody that returns and grows. Orchestra with uilleann pipes, low whistle, fiddle, clàrsach harp, bodhrán, low strings and solo cello. The pre-chorus is a driving waulking-song work chant on bodhrán and hand claps. The bridge is a storm at sea: Highland pipes, pounding timpani, full strings and a chanting choir. Then everything drops to wind, one harp note and a whisper. The final chorus is the biggest moment, sung through tears, and ends on a minor chord, then a lone low whistle.
