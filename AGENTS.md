# Standing artistic direction

Read [docs/production-brief.md](docs/production-brief.md) before pitching, writing, composing, generating art, staging or reviewing any new production or work-in-progress revision.

This is the user's standing direction from 16 November 2026. It governs the story's outcome, the source of any happiness, the music and the characters' actions. The music must always stay on the sad side; visual satire, physical comedy and moments of happiness may exist within the tragedy. Apply the brief before handing off a Suno prompt and again when assessing the ending.
